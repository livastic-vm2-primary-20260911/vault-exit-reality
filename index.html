WHAT YOU ARE ACTUALLY CHECKING

A limit, not a promise.

maxWithdraw(owner) is the standard's declared asset amount allowed through a direct withdrawal. maxRedeem(owner) is the declared redeemable share amount. previewRedeem(shares) estimates assets at the sampled block but intentionally does not enforce withdrawal limits.

Results can become stale before you transact. Some vaults impose asynchronous request/claim flows, pause permissions, permissioned exits, share locks, off-chain intermediaries, cooldowns, or liquidity restrictions. ERC-4626 reads cannot prove any of these are safe or that an exit will execute. Inspect protocol terms, implementation and the current withdrawal queue separately.
