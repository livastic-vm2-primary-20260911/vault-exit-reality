02 Limits deliberately not invented

Owner address: NOT PROVIDED

Owner shares, maxWithdraw(owner), maxRedeem(owner), previewRedeem, and convertToAssets(owner shares) are UNKNOWN / NOT RUN, not zero. Vault accounting totals alone cannot establish any investor's ability to withdraw.

What requires additional verification?

  • Whether direct synchronous withdrawals are enabled for a particular holder.
  • Queues/cooldowns, withdrawals requiring a request/claim lifecycle, and per-owner restrictions.
  • Proxy changes, permission checks, paused exits, slippage/gas and actual redeem transaction behavior.
  • Fresh chain receipts/finality at the time of any *separately authorized* transaction.

ERC-7540 asynchronous vaults can require a separate redemption request and claim. For such vaults, previewRedeem may revert by design; a view-method failure is not proof of theft or guaranteed future liquidity.
